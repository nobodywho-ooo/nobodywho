New binding for .NET 10 on Windows x64, Linux x64/ARM64 and macOS (Apple silicon), published to NuGet as `NobodyWho`. It covers chat with streaming through `await foreach`, tool calling from any delegate, multimodal prompts, sampling, embeddings, reranking, speech-to-text, text-to-speech and voice activity detection. Cancelling a stream stops generation.
